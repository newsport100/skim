SKIM.
Two grids, one shoe. Every card must be placed the instant it's dealt — unless you spend your single, secret skim.
52 cards — a standard deck with the 2♥ and 2♣ swapped out for the Big and Little Joker — are dealt one at a time, alternating between you and the computer. Each card must go face-up into one of your five columns immediately. Once per game, each side may instead skim a card face-down into the discard, unseen. When both grids of 25 are complete, matching columns face off as five-card poker hands — Jokers wild — and whoever wins more columns wins the game.
